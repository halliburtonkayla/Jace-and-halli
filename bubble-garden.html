HALLI’S LITTLE WORLD

Bubble Garden

Meet your bubble buddy

Touch, tickle, pop!

For grown-ups

Simple, open-ended play for Halli. No scores, patterns, or wrong answers.

Blow gently together while touching the screen. Touch grows the bubbles; the microphone is never used.

In the foam, scoop anywhere and swipe to toss. On the board, sweep a finger to pop and draw. Space also plays in every activity.
