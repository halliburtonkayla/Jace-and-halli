Let’s play!

Tap the ground to walk there, or drag your finger like a joystick. Tap equipment to investigate. Tap a running friend to chase; get close enough to tag before they reach the gold base.

As the hider, tap a hiding place to walk there and crouch. Seekers investigate different places. When spotted, run to the gold base. RUN TO BASE finds a route for you. Easy gives you a bigger speed advantage.

Basketball: walk to the ball, pick it up, aim and SHOOT. The centered aim and half power help you hit the basket. You can also swipe upward quickly while holding the ball. Soccer: face the goal and use Dribble or KICK. Turn the goalkeeper off for free practice.

Seesaw: walk to the seat, sit down, and push when low. Your computer friend takes turns. Explore: visit the slide, tunnel, swings or jungle gym, then use the equipment.
