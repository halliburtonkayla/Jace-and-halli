The current background/world design is NOT the visual direction I want.

I do NOT want Jace & Halli’s World to look like a webpage decorated with colored blobs, abstract gradients, floating cards, random shapes, or a flat illustrated background.

Jace & Halli’s World should look like a polished 3D animated children’s game world that Jace and Halli can actually travel through.

The camera/environment should make it possible to feel like they are physically IN the world rather than looking at a decorative webpage.

Use the supplied Jace, Halli, Mommy, and Unique character artwork as the visual-quality/style reference: rounded, colorful, polished 3D animation, soft but detailed materials, child-friendly proportions, dimensional lighting, and cohesive scenery.

DO NOT make the world map/background primarily from CSS blobs, gradients, emoji, clip art, generic cards, or flat colored sections.

Roads must look like roads. Buildings must look like buildings. Grass should look like grass. Trees should be dimensional. The house, school, church, zoo, arcade, bowling alley, circus, train station, store, park, etc. should eventually exist as recognizable physical locations in the same connected world.

Most importantly, do not permanently redesign or destroy working functionality just to correct the visuals. Preserve the systems already being built and replace the visual/environment layer appropriately.

If the current architecture cannot support this level of visual world, explain what needs to change before continuing to decorate the existing blotchy background.

## Visual acceptance checks for future work

- A child can follow a continuous street or sidewalk past recognizable buildings, landscaping and destinations, with camera depth and readable scale.
- Camera movement, occlusion, contact shadows, materials and lighting make the player feel inside the scene. A flat painting beneath interactive controls does not satisfy this.
- Family assets match the correct individual sheet in front, side and back views, including expressions and outfit changes. Reject identity drift rather than accepting a similar generic character.
- Scenery and NPCs share the sheets' rounded, colorful, dimensional animation style. Do not copy the sheet's infographic layout into the game world.
- Preserve code-based family joining, profile progress, assistance, steering, doors, mowing, bubbles, tag and Classic Games while upgrading rendering.
- Show only implemented interactions as playable. Keep missing models, animations and destinations labeled NEEDS ASSET or PLANNED in development records.
- Verify the first neighborhood on real iPad/iPhone Safari before expanding it. A screenshot or a passing simulation test alone is not proof of playable 3D quality.
