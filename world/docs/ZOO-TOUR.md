# Zoo and aquarium tour

The existing approved zoo entrance remains unchanged. Zoo opens zoo.html inside the existing activity frame and returns to the same town/profile. Six connected scenes contain thirty animal hotspots. Next animal crosses section boundaries; the discovery passport persists on this device per profile. No account or paid service is required.

## Artwork

Six original generated illustrations, optimized as WebP in world/assets/zoo. These are stylized illustrations, not wildlife photographs. Each prompt requested one cohesive, warm, polished 3D family-world scene, no people, text, collage, or logos, with five recognizable animals at upper left, upper center, upper right, lower left, lower right. Savanna: giraffe, elephant, zebra, lion, rhino. Rainforest: gorilla, orangutan, tiger, sloth, toucan. Bird Gardens: flamingo, peacock, scarlet macaw, snowy owl, penguin. Reptile River: crocodile, giant tortoise, green tree python, chameleon, tree frog. Coral Reef: clownfish, blue tang, seahorse, octopus, sea turtle in glass exhibits along a curved wooden path. Ocean Tunnel: hammerhead shark, manta ray, moon jellyfish, sea otter and harbor seal in a curved glass tunnel with separate coastal pools.

## Audio and outside videos

Read-aloud uses device speech synthesis after a tap; text remains available without it. Sound effects are synthesized playful imitations and labeled that way. Quiet aquarium animals use habitat water audio, not fabricated animal voices. All audio stops on exit, hiding the page, or video opening. Videos are optional third-party embeds or links; source websites control availability, ads and playback. YouTube segments request 30–60 seconds; some other sources provide live exhibit cameras or video pages instead of a short clip. No video or third-party photo has been copied into the repository.
